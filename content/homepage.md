# About Me

I am a final-year undergraduate majoring in **Geochemistry** and an incoming PhD in **Earth Sciences** with a specialization in **Ore Deposit Geochemistry**, working with Prof. Yang Li at Peking University, Beijing, China. 

My research interests focus on the geochemical, isotopic, and petrological modelings, and my work combines numerical calculations and microprobe analysis to explore the physicochemical processes governing geological and ore-forming systems.
